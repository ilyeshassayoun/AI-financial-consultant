# Pilot Validation Plan

This plan turns product claims into evidence. Do not publish success metrics until the sessions are completed and the raw notes are retained.

## Participants

Recruit 8–12 Germany-based adults across three profiles: first-time planners, active ETF investors, and households within 10 years of retirement. Exclude anyone whose case requires immediate regulated tax, insurance, or investment advice; direct them to a qualified professional.

## Core Tasks

1. Create a household profile and explain the first recommended priority.
2. Find the assumptions behind the tax estimate.
3. Compare two investment strategies and explain P10/P50/P90 in their own words.
4. Identify whether the ETF order basket exactly matches the simulated allocation.
5. Find the retirement contribution currently planned, the total required, and the incremental gap.
6. Export a calculation snapshot and explain what its checksum does—and does not—prove.
7. Leave and resume the journey on a phone-sized viewport.

## Evidence to Capture

- Task completion, time, wrong turns, and facilitator interventions
- Single Ease Question after each task (1–7)
- System Usability Scale after the session
- Misinterpretations of risk, tax, pension, and assurance language
- Performance timings and client/server errors for each failed task

## Release Gates

- At least 85% unassisted completion for tasks 1–6
- No participant interprets modeled outcomes as guarantees
- At least 80% correctly distinguish total from additional retirement saving
- Median task ease at least 5/7; SUS at least 80
- No keyboard trap, clipped action, or unrecoverable navigation failure

Record issues by severity, link each fix to a regression test, and repeat the failed tasks with at least five new participants.
